Hop the pads

Hold Shift to start the engine and let the rotor spool up until the skids lift. Fly to the pad under the amber beacon and set it down. Touch down under 3.5 m/s vertical and 50 km/h forward for a clean landing with no damage.

Mouse control: click the view to capture the mouse; Esc releases it. Mouse movement feeds pitch and roll like a stick that springs back. [ ] change sensitivity. Ctrl isn't used because Ctrl+W closes the browser tab.

It carries momentum. Pitch and roll set a rotation rate, so the aircraft keeps turning after you let go. Neutral collective only just holds you up, so tilting forward to speed up costs altitude unless you add a little. Wait and watch before you correct again. Never brake, turn and descend at the same time.

Assists: Stability damps rotation and holds the attitude you release at. Auto-hover holds altitude when the collective is neutral and brakes to a hover when the stick is centred. Envelope limits cap pitch at 35° and bank at 50°. Turn coordination yaws the nose into banked turns. Switch them off one at a time.

On a touchscreen: left stick for collective and yaw, right stick for pitch and roll.
